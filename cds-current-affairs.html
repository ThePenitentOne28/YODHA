Current Affairs

Monthly framework for CDS preparation

How Much to Cover?

  • Last 6 months – Minimum
  • Last 12 months – Ideal

Focus on relevance, not volume.

What to Focus On (Priority-Based)

  • Defence & Security (High Priority)
    • Military exercises
    • New defence equipment & missiles
    • Appointments & commands
  • National Affairs
    • Government schemes
    • Important bills & acts
    • Reports & indices
  • International Affairs
    • India’s relations with key countries
    • Summits & organisations
  • Science & Technology
    • Space missions
    • Emerging technologies

Recommended Sources

  • Monthly Compilations:
    StudyIQ – Monthly Current Affairs
  • Official & Government Sources:
    • PIB releases (selected)
    • Ministry of Defence updates

Avoid multiple sources. Stick to one primary and revise it well.

Revision Strategy

  • Make short monthly notes
  • Revise each month at least twice
  • Link current affairs with static GK

Revision matters more than reading more sources.
