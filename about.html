About YODHA

A focused platform for defence aspirants

What is YODHA?

YODHA (Youth’s Online Defence Help and Assistance) is a preparation platform built to bring clarity, structure, and focus to defence examination aspirants.

Instead of overwhelming users with scattered material, YODHA emphasises exam-oriented guidance, trusted resources, and a disciplined approach to preparation.

Why YODHA?

  • To reduce confusion caused by excessive and unverified content
  • To provide a structured, priority-based study direction
  • To keep preparation simple, ethical, and focused
  • To build a platform created by aspirants, for aspirants

Vision

The vision of YODHA is to evolve into a reliable digital companion for defence aspirants — guiding them with clarity, discipline, and purpose throughout their preparation journey.

About the Founder

YODHA is built by a defence aspirant who understands the challenges of preparation — information overload, lack of direction, and the need for disciplined guidance.

The platform reflects a belief that serving the nation begins with sincerity, consistency, and responsibility — whether in uniform or through meaningful contributions.
