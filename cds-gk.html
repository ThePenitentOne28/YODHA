General Knowledge

Static + Current Affairs for CDS

→ View Monthly Current Affairs Framework

Static GK (Priority-Based)

  • History (High Priority)
    • Modern India (1857–1947)
    • Freedom Movement & Congress Sessions
    • Governor Generals & Viceroys
  • Polity (High Priority)
    • Indian Constitution
    • Fundamental Rights & Duties
    • President, PM, Parliament, Judiciary
  • Geography (Medium Priority)
    • Indian Physical Geography
    • Rivers, Dams, Soils, Climate
    • World Geography (Basics only)
  • General Science (Medium Priority)
    • Everyday Physics & Chemistry
    • Biology (Human body, diseases)

Focus on NCERT concepts and factual clarity.

Current Affairs (6–12 Months)

  • Defence & Security (High Priority)
    • Military exercises
    • New defence equipment & missiles
    • Appointments in Armed Forces
  • National Affairs
    • Government schemes
    • Important bills & acts
    • Reports & indices
  • International Affairs
    • India’s relations with key countries
    • Summits & organisations
  • Science & Tech
    • Space missions
    • Emerging technologies

Cover last 6–12 months before the exam.

What NOT to Focus On

  • Very old ancient history facts
  • Deep theoretical science
  • Unverified current affairs sources

Focus on relevance, not volume.
