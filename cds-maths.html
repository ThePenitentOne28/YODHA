Mathematics

Concepts → Practice → Accuracy

How to Approach Mathematics for CDS

  • Build strong basics before attempting speed
  • Memorise formulas and standard results
  • Practice topic-wise before mixed questions
  • Avoid advanced theory not asked in CDS

CDS Mathematics tests clarity, accuracy, and time management.

  • Arithmetic (High Priority)
    • Percentage (increase/decrease, comparison)
    • Ratio & Proportion
    • Average & Mixture
    • Time & Work
    • Pipes & Cisterns
    • Time, Speed & Distance
    • Profit, Loss & Discount
    • Simple & Compound Interest
  • Algebra (Equations, Inequalities)
  • Trigonometry (Medium Priority)
    • Trigonometric ratios
    • Standard identities
    • Heights & Distances
    • Basic trigonometric equations
  • Geometry & Mensuration
  • Statistics

Topic-wise practice and references will be added soon.

Formula Focus

  • Maintain a separate formula notebook
  • Revise formulas weekly
  • Practice 5–10 questions per formula

Most CDS maths questions are formula-based.

What NOT to Focus On

  • Advanced calculus
  • Engineering mathematics
  • Complex number theory
  • Very lengthy derivations

CDS mathematics is about speed and accuracy, not depth.
