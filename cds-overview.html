CDS Examination Overview

Understanding the exam before preparing for it

What is CDS?

The Combined Defence Services (CDS) Examination is conducted by UPSC to select officers for the Indian Armed Forces through a written examination followed by SSB interview.

Exam Structure

  • Written Examination (Objective type)
  • SSB Interview (Personality & Intelligence)
  • Medical Examination

Written Examination Papers

  • English – 100 marks
  • General Knowledge – 100 marks
  • Mathematics – 100 marks

(IMA / INA / AFA candidates appear for all three papers)

Preparation Approach

  • Understand syllabus and exam trend
  • Build strong basics before speed
  • Practice questions consistently
  • Revise and analyse mistakes
